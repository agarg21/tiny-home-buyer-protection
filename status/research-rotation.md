# Human-Value Research Rotation

Last updated: 2026-08-13

This is the due-work view for research during the 90-day publication hold. It
is not a content calendar. Each run starts with one real reader question,
reviews one bounded surface, and ends with `keep`, `repair`, `consolidate`,
`needs-evidence`, `draft-answer`, or `no-build`. A run may promote at most one
future action and never edits a public page unless that later action is
separately registered and reviewed.

## Selection Order

1. A verified production, technical, indexing, privacy, or trust defect.
2. An unresolved prior human-review finding that has not been rechecked against
   the current rendered page.
3. A material official-source freshness risk.
4. A repeated query/page or community-question pattern that exposes a specific
   unmet decision job.
5. A page-product overlap or internal handoff that may deserve consolidation.

Low impressions, a calendar slot, or the age of a page alone are not enough.
Unchanged GSC blocks speculative rewriting, not bounded research whose due
reason is already durable.

## Community Question Lane

Community research is qualitative evidence and service, not link building. A
candidate must be recent enough to help, tightly matched to something we know,
and answerable without pretending to have personal experience or local facts
we do not have.

The current candidate is the July 29 Reddit question about estimating the

`docs/research/community-question-candidates-2026-08.md`. It remains
`draft-only`. Any exact post, comment, vote, message, account action, or site
link requires the user's approval first.

## Completion Rule

A research run is complete when it preserves the question, sources, persona
and section findings, unknowns, and one disposition. `Keep` and `no-build` are
useful results. Do not repeat the same review while its inputs and conclusion
remain fresh.
