# Community Answer Review Prompt

Use this before a Tiny Home Clarity answer is offered for posting on Reddit or
another public community. The default transaction is research and drafting
only. The user must approve the exact answer and destination before any post,
comment, vote, message, link insertion, or account action.

## Question Fit

- What is the person actually trying to decide or do?
- Which facts did they provide, and which location, home, parcel, use, budget,
  or transaction details remain unknown?
- Can we answer the useful part now without inventing local facts or personal
  experience?
- Is the thread recent and still likely to benefit the original poster?

Reject the candidate when the best answer would be generic sympathy, a search
result summary, a vendor recommendation, a legal conclusion, an unsupported
cost range, or a link to our site.

## Standalone Value

The answer must work if every Tiny Home Clarity reference is removed. It should:

1. answer the actual question in the first sentence or two;
2. explain the most important distinction in plain language;
3. give a short ordered next step, checklist, calculation method, or exact
   questions to ask;
4. identify the few missing facts that would materially change the answer;
5. state uncertainty once, then narrow it instead of repeating caveats; and
6. avoid language that could be pasted under an unrelated housing question.

Fail at P2 when the answer is generic, promotional, topic-swappable, padded,
or unable to give a concrete next action.

## Human Voice

- Write like one informed person helping another, not a brand, article, policy
  memo, or SEO page.
- Use short paragraphs and ordinary words.
- Do not claim first-hand experience, professional credentials, or outcomes we
  do not have.
- Do not imitate slang, force friendliness, or overuse headings and bullets.
- Do not scold the questioner for missing information.

## Trust And Links

- Do not state that a tiny home is legal, buildable, code-compliant, insurable,
  financeable, or complete at a given price without adequate evidence.
- Do not recommend a builder, seller, community, lender, insurer, or vendor.
- Prefer official sources for local rules, safety, money, and code claims.
- A site link is optional, never the point of the answer, and must save the
  reader meaningful work beyond the standalone response.
- If a Tiny Home Clarity link is included, disclose the relationship plainly in
  the same paragraph. Do not use disguised promotion or manufacture engagement.

## Required Verdict

Return one of:

- `PASS_FOR_USER_APPROVAL`: the exact answer is useful, human, safe, and ready
  for the user to decide whether to post.
- `REVISE`: list concrete P0-P3 findings and bounded fixes.
- `NO_ANSWER`: we cannot add enough trustworthy value.

Also record the exact destination URL, review date, sources used, whether a
link is included, the disclosure text if applicable, and every unresolved
fact. A pass is not posting authority.
